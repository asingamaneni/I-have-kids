---
name: evolve-learning-graph
description: "Propose a validated subject-graph extension or evidence-backed practice branch without activating it."
whenToUse: When a learner reaches a roadmap frontier, an adult adds a subject or goal, or repeated confirmed evidence calls for another practice route.
argumentHint: "[student-id] [subject] [goal-or-frontier]"
model: inherit
effort: high
---

Grow the roadmap from evidence, not from grade labels or guesswork.

1. Load the learner profile, adult goals, active curriculum revisions, the complete top-to-bottom adult subject map, confirmed concept evidence, due reviews, and repeated mistake tags through local MCP tools. Locate the learner inside the whole progression, not just the currently visible child horizon.
2. Decide whether the need is a learner-specific extra-practice/reassessment directive on the selected graph node, an approved successor concept, an interest extension, or a genuinely new curriculum revision. Apply learner-specific practice without changing the shared curriculum; reuse an active concept whenever it already covers the need.
3. For a new revision, preserve every historical concept ID. Add stable subject/concept IDs, typed edges, ordered learning stages, registered generator/evaluator/renderer IDs, original activity templates, answer specifications, provenance, and a plain-language rationale. Age and school placement may adapt presentation but never cap eligibility.
4. Call the deterministic curriculum-proposal tool. Fix every duplicate, dangling edge, cycle, unsupported item kind, missing answer, or historical-compatibility error before presenting the result.
5. Show the adult a concise graph diff: added nodes and edges, affected learners, why the change fits this child, and which evidence or goal prompted it.
6. Stop after storing the proposal. Never call approval or activation tools on a proposal you authored. An adult must review it in the curriculum view or explicitly approve it through the corresponding local tool.
